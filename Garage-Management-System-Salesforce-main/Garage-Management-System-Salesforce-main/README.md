## 📘 Project Overview

The **Garage Management System** is a Salesforce-based application that streamlines the operations of automotive service centers. It helps manage customers, appointments, service records, and billing efficiently using Salesforce’s cloud platform.

This system enhances productivity, transparency, and customer satisfaction by automating manual workflows and providing real-time insights into business operations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Manage customer details and appointments effectively
* Maintain service and billing records seamlessly
* Automate notifications and service updates
* Improve data accessibility and reduce manual errors

---

## ⚙️ Technologies Used

* **Platform:** Salesforce (Cloud CRM)
* **Tools:** Flow Builder, Apex Triggers, Validation Rules, Reports & Dashboards
* **Languages:** Apex, SOQL, JavaScript (Lightning Components)

---

## 🧩 Features

* Customer and Appointment Management
* Service Record Tracking
* Automated Billing and Feedback
* Role-based Access Control
* Reports & Dashboards for Insights
* Data Validation and Duplication Rules

---

## 🧱 Architecture

* **Frontend:** Salesforce Lightning Experience
* **Backend:** Apex Classes, Triggers, and Workflows
* **Database:** Salesforce Cloud Data Storage
